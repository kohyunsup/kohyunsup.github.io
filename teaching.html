Teaching

My goal as a teacher is to help students understand course material and develop their own ideas about it in speech and writing. I teach academic reading and writing directly rather than assuming students arrive able to do them. I connect abstract theory to a case students already follow. I set ground rules that make stating and defending a claim both expected and safe. And I assess reasoning rather than fluency.

Courses

Instructor of record. Introduction to International Relations, summer 2026. I designed the syllabus, all class materials, and all assessments. The course opens with a section on reading scholarship and writing in the social sciences before any theory is introduced, and closes with a case study applying each paradigm to a single current conflict.

Discussion sections. Four semesters of Introduction to International Relations, and Introduction to Political Analysis in the current semester. I plan these meetings myself and wrote the section syllabus for the course I am teaching now.

Upper-division courses. International Security, the Israeli–Palestinian Conflict, and Fighting Crime in Latin America, as teaching assistant. I have given invited guest lectures in International Security, Fighting Crime in Latin America, and Introduction to International Relations.
